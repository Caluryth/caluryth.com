Background & Involvement

Callum is a high school student with a strong interest in applied technology and civic engagement.

He serves as a treasurer on his school's student council, leads and mentors with the Royal Canadian Sea Cadets, and is a member of the Students Commission of Canada Youth Roster.

On the technical side, he builds desktop applications, mobile apps, and automation tools — driven by a preference for learning by doing.
