About

Callum Haley is a high school student with a serious interest in applied technology and civic engagement.

He builds real software — desktop applications, mobile apps, automation tools — driven by a preference for learning by doing rather than waiting until he's "ready."

Outside of code, he leads and mentors with the Royal Canadian Sea Cadets, serves as Student Council Treasurer, and engages nationally through the Students Commission of Canada.
